## Jupiter 125
Ask-words: Jupiter, scooter, family, price, mileage, storage, helmet, colors
Type: scooter, 125cc
Price: starts under Rs 90,000 ex-showroom, varies by variant and city
Variants: Drum Alloy, Disc, DT SXC, SmartXonnect
Mileage: about 50 km per litre (ARAI claimed)
Highlights: 33-litre under-seat storage that TVS says fits two helmets, front fuel-filling without getting off, metal body, USB charging
Best for: family and daily city use

## Raider 125
Ask-words: Raider, motorcycle, bike, sporty, mileage, price, ride mode, youth
Type: motorcycle, 125cc, 7 variants
Price: starts under Rs 90,000 ex-showroom, top variants go to about Rs 1 lakh
Mileage: about 56.7 km per litre (ARAI claimed)
Highlights: ride modes (Eco and Power, Boost on higher variants), 5-inch TFT display and SmartXonnect on top variants, dual disc brakes with single-channel ABS on the SXC DD and TFT DD variants
Top speed: about 99 km/h
Best for: daily commute with a sporty look

## iQube Electric
Ask-words: iQube, electric, EV, range, charging, running cost, battery, subsidy, petrol bachat
Type: electric scooter, variants from 2.3 kWh up to 5.3 kWh
Price: depends on variant and city. Central PM E-Drive subsidy is up to Rs 5,000, and some states add their own.
Do not quote an exact price. Say: "Price depends on variant and city, our team will confirm the exact amount."
Range: varies by variant, about 100 km or more per charge on the base model (claimed)
Running cost: roughly 30 paise per km at home charging rates (estimate)
Highlights: silent ride, reverse assist, smart display, geo-fencing, live battery status
Best for: city commuting with low running cost
